# Hook Optimizer

**Purpose.** Choose hooks based on reader value, content proof and voice, without misleading curiosity gaps.

**Trigger and ownership.** When a draft needs opening options or the hook fails editorial review.

**Inputs.** Core content promise, actual proof, audience, platform, voice profile and intended format.

## Required process

1. Identify the single promise and the proof the post will actually deliver. If weak, strengthen substance before hook tactics.
2. Generate 4–6 distinct opening mechanisms: specific observation, unexpected lesson, concrete result (only if verified), question, story tension or useful checklist.
3. Match opening length/visual density to platform. Instagram slide 1 needs instant visual orientation; LinkedIn line 1 should explain stakes; X must be concise.
4. Annotate each option with the promised payoff and any factual claims requiring a source.
5. Reject manufactured numbers, fake controversy, clickbait that cannot be fulfilled, and formulaic AI contrast reveals.
6. Select with creator voice and actual reader need; pass chosen hook to the writer and final QA.
