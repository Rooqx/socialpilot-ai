# Publish-readiness is a gated decision, not a score

## Hard gates (any failure blocks review-pass)

1. **Request adherence:** right number of outputs, target platform, format, topic and audience.
2. **Grounding:** external facts sourced, personal experiences creator-confirmed, no fabricated clients/achievements/performance.
3. **Semantic integrity:** body fulfills the hook; ending doesn't promise absent materials or links.
4. **Platform:** current constraints verified where required, appropriate account capability understood, accessible format.
5. **Assets:** every required image/video exists, matches caption and passed visual inspection—or output is explicitly a text-only draft/brief.
6. **Safety and rights:** no copied private content, unlicensed visuals, manipulative engagement schemes or exposure of credentials.
7. **Ownership:** the creator's approval is not inferred from a passing editorial checklist.

## Scoring after gates

For eligible drafts, score **clarity, usefulness, voice fidelity, specific evidence, reader relevance** using brief qualitative notes or a transparent rubric. This is *editorial judgment*, not a prediction of reach or a substitute for measured analytics. Do not average away a hard failure.

## Example verdicts

- A useful, grounded X post with all constraints checked: `pass_for_user_review` (not published).
- An Instagram caption with a storyboard but no rendered carousel: `blocked_for_asset` unless the user requested caption-only output.
- A LinkedIn post claiming "I led a team of 40" without creator evidence: `blocked_for_claim`.
- A Reel script requested as a script, with asset dependencies labeled: eligible as a **script deliverable**, not a finished video.

## Regression tests

Rewrite approved text after QA and verify previous approval is invalidated. Confirm a source-date change triggers re-verification. Do not falsely certify platform compliance from an undated local playbook. If a draft has unresolved facts, provide a small actionable correction list rather than a made-up score.
