# Technique-by-technique source fidelity — community-engagement

This ledger maps **re-authored editorial/operational techniques** to their upstream intellectual sources. It is not a claim of verbatim import or exhaustive preservation of every upstream sentence. Each method is reviewed for portability, current platform evidence, and risks.

| Method | Specialist implementation | Origin / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| M01 Context before response | Read the actual public comment or owner-provided excerpt and identify whether a reply should inform, thank, correct, disagree respectfully or not respond | Corey social engagement strategy |
| M02 Value over bait | Provide useful facts, relevant experience and an optional question; avoid mass templates, aggressive sales links or fake participation | Charlie pinned-comment and Corey social |
| M03 Priority triage | Escalate safety, privacy, accusations and account security issues to the owner; never claim to resolve a dispute from incomplete evidence | Corey community-marketing triage |
| M04 Human approval | Draft suggested replies and moderator notes | Corey loops publishing guardrails |
| M05 Feedback to ideas | Capture repeated thoughtful objections as content improvement opportunities, keeping identities anonymized when appropriate | Corey customer-research source synthesis |
