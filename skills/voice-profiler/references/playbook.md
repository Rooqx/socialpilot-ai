# Evidence-based voice calibration

## Measure recognizable choices, not vague adjectives

For each of 3–5 consented writing samples, capture platform, intended audience, purpose, estimated formality, sentence length range, paragraph rhythm, point of view, typical opening, specific jargon and common ending. Store the observations as evidence and identify contradictions. Treat sample quirks and typos as possible noise, not mandatory style rules.

Turn observations into operational pairs: **Do:** "start with a concrete engineering tradeoff"; **Avoid:** "generic lessons with no example". A rule like "authentic tone" is not testable until supported by a worked rewrite. Assign `verified` only to a voice supported by genuine samples, otherwise `provisional`.

## Calibration protocol

1. Start from existing confirmed `voice.json`; request missing samples only if quality depends on them.
2. Separate subject-matter expertise, factual persona and prose style; matching voice never authorizes invented experiences.
3. Produce at least two short excerpts: one natural positive model and one clearly overpolished anti-example, with specific rationale.
4. Adapt one concept to X, LinkedIn and Instagram without flattening every platform into identical tone.
5. Ask the creator to choose or edit a version; save confirmed rules and cite the user-owned examples that informed them.
6. After several approved posts, assess whether edits reflect consistent preferences or isolated context; change the profile only with owner confirmation.

## Worked example

**Observed sample trait:** opens with a concrete problem and uses short paragraphs to explain a fix. **Poor imitation:** "In today's fast-paced tech landscape, innovation is key." **Better provisional example:** "Our deployment failed because the environment variable existed locally but not in production. Here's the check I now run before a release." Use the second only when the technical incident is independently confirmed as the author's experience; otherwise present it as a clearly labeled fictional demonstration.

## Failure tests

No samples available; samples copied from another creator; mixed-author sample pack; strong differences between personal and company profiles; sensitive anecdotes present in source; assistant overfitting to one sentence; outdated voice rules overriding direct current user preference.
