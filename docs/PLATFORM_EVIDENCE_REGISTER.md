## Rejected unsupported upstream prescriptions

Do **not** carry the following as facts into new drafts or strategy advice unless newly supported by verifiable official data: a universal numerical X reply-to-like multiplier, exactly 50% reach loss for links, a blanket 30-minute threshold that decides every post's lifetime, fixed post lengths claimed to be algorithmically optimal, magic hashtag counts, or a guarantee that a chosen format will yield more followers.

X's public system is a **recommendation architecture**, not a blueprint for manipulating users. Our legitimate use is to make useful content relevant, trustworthy and readable to target audiences. Good outputs do not guarantee growth; track actual results and revise hypotheses.
