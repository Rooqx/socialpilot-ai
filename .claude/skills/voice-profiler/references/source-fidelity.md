# Technique-by-technique source fidelity — voice-profiler

This ledger maps **re-authored editorial/operational techniques** to their upstream intellectual sources. It is not a claim of verbatim import or exhaustive preservation of every upstream sentence. Each method is reviewed for portability, current platform evidence, and risks.

| Method | Specialist implementation | Origin / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| M01 Consent and identity | Use three to five owner-provided writing samples where possible, record their platform, date and provenance, and verify they represent the intended author | Charlie voice-builder sample-based interview |
| M02 Rhythm fingerprint | Extract common sentence lengths, paragraph pacing, typical use of fragments, contractions, punctuation, technical specificity and first-person stance | Charlie voice-builder detailed sample analysis |
| M03 Open/close signatures | Inventory credible opening patterns, transition types, evidence style and endings | Charlie voice-builder voice.md sections |
| M04 Context-sensitive voice | Keep one underlying author identity while changing formality and compression by X, LinkedIn or Instagram; never force professional LinkedIn formatting onto a casual X reply | BlackTwist voice matching guidance |
