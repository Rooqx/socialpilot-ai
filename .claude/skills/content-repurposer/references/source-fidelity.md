# Technique-by-technique source fidelity — content-repurposer

This ledger maps **re-authored editorial/operational techniques** to their upstream intellectual sources. It is not a claim of verbatim import or exhaustive preservation of every upstream sentence. Each method is reviewed for portability, current platform evidence, and risks.

| Method | Specialist implementation | Origin / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| M01 Content atoms | Extract distinct defensible facts, insights, anecdotes, quotes (with permissions), demonstrations and questions from the source | Corey social content atoms and BlackTwist repurposing |
| M02 Standalone quality | Check each atom has enough context to educate or entertain by itself; do not publish a derivative that relies on the reader seeing the original material | BlackTwist repurposer process |
| M03 Angle differentiation | Translate one insight into an X concise finding, a LinkedIn professional decision, and an Instagram visual sequence rather than rewriting identical copy for every platform | BlackTwist repurposing matrix and Corey distribution |
| M04 Source integrity | Never alter the meaning of a statistic, remove a critical caveat, imply ownership of an external case or republish copyrighted assets without permission | Corey copy-editing and research |
| M05 Tracking and scheduling | Link derivatives by common parent idea ID and record whether each format has already been drafted, published or queued | Corey loops idempotency, BlackTwist leverage ranking |
