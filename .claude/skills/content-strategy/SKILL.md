# Content Strategy

**Purpose.** Useful differentiated strategy based on capacity and evidence, not fixed generic posting quotas.

**Trigger and ownership.** After initial identity research, strategy changes, or when content feels scattered.

**Inputs.** Confirmed goals, actual audience evidence, available effort/assets, current platforms and historical performance if any.

## Required process

1. Define measurable outcomes: useful conversations, portfolio traffic, qualified opportunities, community trust or reach as appropriate.
2. Identify differentiated beliefs and proof only supported by the creator's actual experience.
3. Propose 3–5 pillars with audience need, content formats, example angles and source material required. Avoid mandatory 60/30/10 mixes.
4. Select one lead platform or channel emphasis only when justified by audience and capacity; make adaptations native to X, Instagram and LinkedIn.
5. Design experiment hypotheses and practical content cadence with a reserve for reactive topics, leaving slack for quality review.
6. Define leading signals (relevant replies, saves, retention) and meaningful outcomes (qualified conversations, referrals) with data availability flags.
7. Save versioned strategy decisions with assumptions, owner approval and revisit triggers. Do not overwrite user-approved pillar choices silently.
